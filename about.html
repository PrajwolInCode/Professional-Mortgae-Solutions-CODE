About Prabhat

Prabhat Silwal is an Australian mortgage broker and property investor known for providing clear, strategic credit advice tailored to each client’s financial position and long-term objectives.

He works closely with individuals, families, and investors to understand their goals, timeframe, and broader financial plans before presenting lending options that align with their circumstances. His approach is deliberate and structured — focused on clarity, thoughtful loan structuring, and a seamless experience from consultation through to settlement.

A Strategic Approach to Lending

Every lending decision has long-term implications. Prabhat’s focus extends beyond interest rates to consider structure, flexibility, and future planning.

With access to a broad panel of lenders, he provides carefully considered options and clear explanations of features, costs, and key considerations — enabling clients to make informed decisions with confidence.

As an active property investor, he understands lending from both a professional and personal perspective, particularly for clients building or refining property portfolios.
