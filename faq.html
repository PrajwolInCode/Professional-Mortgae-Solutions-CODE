To guide your loan application, we typically request:
  • Identification: Passport, driver’s licence, or birth certificate
  • Income evidence: Payslips, tax returns, or accountant-prepared statements (for self-employed)
  • Bank statements: Usually the last 3–6 months
  • Existing liabilities: Credit cards, personal loans, or other debts
  • Current loan statements: Mortgage, car loans, or investment loans
  • Property details: If refinancing or buying, details of the property and rates notices
  • Business documents: For business or SMSF loans — company financials, trust deeds, or SMSF statements
  • Other supporting information: Any additional documents relevant to your situation

We provide a tailored checklist specific to your circumstances, so you know exactly what’s required.

Timeframes vary depending on the lender and how quickly we receive your documents. As a guide:
  • Simple applications (employed with straightforward income): Typically 24–72 hours once documents are submitted
  • Self-employed or complex applications (SMSF, multiple properties, investment portfolios): Often 3–7 business days
  • Refinancing or top-up loans: Usually 1–5 business days

Once your documents are complete, we lodge your application promptly and keep you informed at every stage, so you have a clear idea of the typical process.
